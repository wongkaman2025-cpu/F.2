📚 孝言的二手書計劃 / Jason's Used Textbook Plan

情境 Scenario：
孝言（Jason）是一名中六學生。每逢暑假，他發現自己以極低的便宜價格（原價的一成或每公斤 $5）將保養良好的舊課本賣給二手書店。然而，二手書店再轉售這些舊書時，售價卻高達原價的六成至八成！這觸發了他自己經營二手課本生意的點子。
Jason is a Form 6 student. Every summer, he sells his well-conditioned used textbooks to old book stores at a very low price (10% of the original price or $5/kg). However, the resale value of these books at the stores is high (60% to 80% of original price). This triggers his idea to start a used textbook business.

📝 第二部分：開設業務的規劃過程六個步驟

開設一項業務需要有一份業務計劃 (Business Plan)，它是創業的導向圖 (roadmap)。To set up a business, you need a Business Plan, which serves as a roadmap.

1

設定目標 / Setting Objectives

  • 賺取最多利潤 To maximize the profits earned
  • 成為市場最大 To be the largest share of the market
  • 服務最多的顧客 To maximize the number of customers served
2

了解市場 / Understanding the Market

  • 附近有沒有售賣二手課本的書店？（有 / 無）
    Are there used bookstores nearby?
  • 這些書店是大型還是小型？
    Are these stores big or small?
  • 這些書店的書售價便宜還是昂貴？
    Are their books cheap or expensive?
3

決定銷售策略 / Deciding Selling Strategy

  • 目標貨品：銷售初中、高中，還是全部的二手課本？
    Selling junior, senior, or all forms of used textbooks?
  • 主打科目：以售賣哪些科目的二手課本為主？
    Which subjects to focus on selling?
4

組織業務架構 / Organizing the Business

  • 企業形式：以獨資經營、合夥，還是有限公司形式經營？
    Operate as a sole proprietorship, partnership, or limited company?
  • 經營渠道：採用實體店形式，還是透過網上平台形式經營？
    A physical brick-and-mortar presence, or an e-Shop through the internet?
5

製作財務預算 / Making Financial Budgets

  • 銷量估算：預期銷售量及滯銷書量。
    Expected sales and unsold books.
  • 成本預估：預期每本二手書的成本。
    Expected cost per used textbook.
  • 定價原則：預期每本二手書的售價。
    Expected price per used textbook.
  • 利潤預測：預期可賺取多少利潤。
    Expected profit to be earned.
6

採取行動 / Taking Action

  • 開始經營：當一切準備就緒，便可開始經營業務。
    When everything is ready, start the business.
  • 推廣宣傳：在經營過程中進行宣傳，以增加店舖的知名度，從而增加利潤。
    Conduct promotion during operation to increase publicity and profits.
